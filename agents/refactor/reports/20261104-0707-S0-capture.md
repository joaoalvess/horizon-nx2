## Finding 1 (R2): DK's ASTC copy destination

Before R2, every DK capture logged `[RED] DM_NVN_CAPTURE: texture ser=… 512x512 format 194 … content not captured (format the capture cannot read back)`. The texture is ASTC 8x8 sRGB: `cap_pf_block` in `nvn_capture.inc` knew plain texels and BC only. Its replay copy stayed undefined, so DK's 3D frames came out magenta (4.3-4.7 M px).

With R2 the line is gone: `dk_save` reads back 64 textures instead of 63, and the magenta is gone. The `dk_save` banana icon still differed by the same 1,096 px, with the same digest `7371210b`. So my earlier reading, that the icon was that texture, was wrong: the icon was finding 2.

## Finding 2 (R3): the replayer deswizzled Switch 2 textures with Switch 1's GOB order

This was the root cause of every Pokopia 3D mismatch and of DK's whole-frame blockiness.

Symptom, before R3:
- `pk_world`, `pk_walk`, `pk_menu` and `pk_bag` differed by 324-607 k px.
- Every replay was identical to every other one, and the pools after the frame equalled live.

How it was found (`pk_diag2`: live `DM_NVN_TEXDUMP=4199` + `SNAPRAW` + `TEXRAW=8175` + `TEXINFO=4x4` + `PASSDUMP`, against `--texdump` of the replay):
- Passes 1-17 write identical targets.
- The first target that differs is pass 18's `8177`, an R8 768x432 SSAO target, and then the pass-19 blur `8178`. They differ only on rows y%4 = 2 and 3, by 1-5 levels.
- The later targets (8176, 5435, 8194, 5828) carry the same pattern on a 10-row period, i.e. 432 rows scaled up to 1080.
- Pass 18 samples ser 8175, a 4x4 RGBA8 noise texture with view swizzle BGRA. Its first resolution is `swizzled=1`: it was CPU-written (WriteTexels or packaged data) before first use. Its storage is 512 bytes with 31 non-zero bytes, in live and in the replay alike.
- `TEXRAW` of the GPU texture:
  - Live: 4 full rows, whose non-zero bytes are exactly those 31.
  - Replay: rows 0-1 identical, rows 2-3 all zero.
- In the Switch 2 GOB order, the rows of a 16-byte-wide texture sit at bytes 0/16/32/48 (`nvn_deswizzle.c`: `(y%8/4)*128 + (x%32/16)*64 + (y%4/2)*32 + (y%2)*16`). In the Switch 1 order, rows 2-3 sit at 64/80, which are zero here. This matches the replay exactly.
- Code:
  - `nvn_gob_switch2` is set only in `nvnDeviceInitialize` (`hle_nvn.c:127`), from `dm_sdk_ounce()`.
  - `nvn_replay` links the weak `dm_sdk_ounce()` from `hle_nvn.c:184`, which returns 0. `nm -m` shows "weak external"; dmhost links the strong one from `loader.c:151`.
  - The capture recorded no such fact.
  - The same stub also drops the NVN2 compute dispatch dims in c0 (`nvn_mtl.m:8173`, `10897`).

Result with R3 (`cand-R3/nvn_replay --sdk-ounce 1`):
- All 7 Pokopia captures are bit-exact, the 3D ones included, with no recapture.
- `dk_save` is exact: the banana icon was a storage-uploaded texture in the wrong GOB order.
- DK's 3D frames went from about 4.3 M px to 0-7 k px.

## Finding 3: what DK still has after R3 (open)

- **Warm replays (`dk_neon`, `dk_cliff`)**:
  - Every fresh replay is exact, and warm replay 1 is exact. Warm replays 2 and 3 equal each other and differ from live: 1,314 px with max byte 2 for neon (box x 2129-2535, y 1379-1470 of 2880x1620), and 4,556 px for cliff.
  - The only texture a warm replay does not restore is the one the frame makes with no captured content (`--made`): ser 4624, RG11B10 2304x1296, the lit HDR target.
  - `--warm 3 --texdump` on neon, replay 1 against replay 2:
    - 4624 differs in every pixel.
    - The bloom chain (33060, 33062, 21888, 33066, 33068, 21897, 21963) differs in 2-17 px, in the same screen region as the final difference.
    - Every other target is identical.
    - Replays 2 and 3 are identical in every target.
  - In the frame:
    - Pass 2 clears all of 4624.
    - Pass 94, compute `5e0110401b4a3d8f`, reads bloom 21963 and writes 4624 as an image.
    - In replay 1 the host first logs "texture upgraded to allow image writes ser=4624" at that point. Replays 2 and 3 start with the upgraded texture.
  - So yes: 4624 explains the DK warm failures. It is the carried-over state that 4624 brings into a warm replay, not its content, since pass 2 clears it.
  - Why the texture's prior state changes the frame's output is open. The full-frame 4624 difference in the replay-1 dump may be partly an artefact of the upgrade (the dump list may hold the pre-upgrade object).
  - Possible fixes: the capture reads 4624 back, or the replayer recreates frame-made textures for each warm replay.
- **Fresh replays that differ from each other (`dk_isle` 5/8 exact, `dk_mine` 4/8, `dk_ypunch` 0/8 with 11 digests in 11 replays)**:
  - 4624 cannot explain these, since every fresh replay starts from the same state.
  - `dk_ypunch` also writes 7 pool blocks after the frame that live did not write (41 before R3).
  - Open: a GPU ordering or race in the replay, not investigated.
- Before R2, `dk_isle`'s replays also differed from each other by about 30 px. That fits the same open nondeterminism.
